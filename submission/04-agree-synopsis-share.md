# Field 04 — Synopsis sharing acknowledgement

**Form section:** Component 1a → 2 - Project Description → A
**Field label:** *"By checking this box, you acknowledge that your project synopsis may be shared with various organizations and stakeholders within the defence & security community, innovation partners, and/or Other Government Departments."*
**Type:** Radio: `I Agree` / `I Do Not Agree`
**Status:** ✅ READY

## Select

**`I Agree`**

## Why

- IDEaS publishes funded project synopses publicly; this is part of how the program builds the innovation ecosystem.
- Saying "I Do Not Agree" likely disqualifies or hides the proposal from the very stakeholders we want to reach.
- The synopsis content (Field 03) is written for public release — nothing in it is sensitive.
