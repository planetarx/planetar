## Why TRL-2

- The solution is the learned, self-supervised cross-modal fusion model — the AI that learns to re-identify dark vessels. It is the new research: formulated as a concept with a concrete architecture and strong component-level evidence, but not yet built or demonstrated. That is TRL-2.
- Component evidence behind the concept: the per-modality encoders already exist as four working detectors; the self-supervision signal (AIS co-occurrence) is identified; and the applicant's peer-reviewed semi-supervised deep learning on Ocean Networks Canada hydrophone data (95% accuracy) shows the method transfers to this regime.
- The built substrate (bus, envelope, entity graph, analyst console) is enabling infrastructure that makes the model feasible to build and demonstrate — it is not itself the solution, so it does not set the solution's TRL.

## Why not TRL-1 or TRL-3

- TRL-1 under-sells: this is beyond basic principles — a concrete architecture with component evidence is formulated.
- TRL-3 over-claims at start: the integrated fusion model is not yet demonstrated. Demonstrating it on public data and live at planetar.ca is the project's end-state (Field 10 = TRL-3).
