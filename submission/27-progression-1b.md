Zax Analytics intends to progress this solution into Component 1b (Develop, TRL 4-5) and beyond. Component 1a delivers a demonstrated proof-of-concept at TRL 3: a trained, calibrated, self-supervised cross-modal fusion model that re-identifies dark vessels — vessels that disable their AIS transponders to evade tracking — by fusing AIS, synthetic-aperture radar, electro-optical imagery, passive acoustics, radio-frequency emissions and textual maritime reports. It is validated on public data and operable live at planetar.ca, where a Technical Authority can drive it during evaluation.
S&T work under Component 1b advances the capability from proof-of-concept to operationally-relevant maturity along four lines.
1) Productionize and harden the model. We move from public and synthetic data to operationally representative streams; harden ingest, monitoring and graceful degradation when sensors drop; and add robustness to adversarial behaviour such as AIS spoofing and identity swapping. The human-in-the-loop adjudication captured in 1a becomes an online active-learning loop, so operator corrections continuously refine the model — a Collaborative Intelligence design in which the analyst is empowered by the AI and the AI improves from the analyst.
2) Operational validation with a CAF partner. We evaluate dark-vessel re-identification against operator-defined scenarios and metrics on representative data in a controlled environment (TRL 4), then in an operationally relevant environment (TRL 5). Uncertainty remains conformally calibrated and every output remains traceable to its inputs, so results are accreditable rather than opaque.
3) Extend to a second multi-domain setting. The core idea — using an intermittent strong identifier (AIS, here) to self-supervise the other modalities — generalizes without redesign. The same pattern applies to Joint ISR for Arctic operations (satellite imagery, RF and telemetry) and to airborne multi-sensor platforms (radar, EO/IR and telemetry for detecting stealth or spoofed assets), broadening from the maritime beachhead to multi-domain situational awareness.
4) Edge deployment and accreditation. We adapt the perception front-end for size-, weight- and power-constrained tactical-edge hardware (the Edge Fusion for Tactical Units use case) and degraded-connectivity operation; add multi-level classification handling so the system operates across security levels with full lineage; and begin a formal accreditation / Authority-to-Operate readiness package.
End-state of Component 1b: a validated, operationally-relevant cross-modal fusion capability at TRL 5 — component and system validated in a relevant environment — delivering calibrated, explainable, policy-aware multi-domain situational awareness across maritime and at least one additional domain, with an accreditation-readiness package and a clear path to TRL 6+ operational deployment.
Capabilities and benefits for Canada. The capability restores track continuity on vessels that deliberately evade monitoring, directly supporting maritime domain awareness against illegal fishing, sanctions evasion, ship-to-ship transfers and Arctic sovereignty incursions. It increases the speed of decision-making by sharing the load between AI correlation and human adjudication, and reduces system vulnerability by replacing brittle, siloed, single-modality pipelines with one auditable, provenance-tracked fusion model. It aligns with the DND/CAF AI Strategy and Force Capability Plan priorities for ISR, C2 and operational resilience. Because it is grounded in the applicant's own open-source code and new, Canadian-owned IP, and is open-source-replicable on commodity hardware, it is a sovereign Canadian capability that interoperates with allies (NATO and Five-Eyes maritime domain awareness).
Advancement to market and commercialization. The capability is dual-use. Beyond the CAF, the same cross-modal fusion and entity-resolution engine serves civil maritime domain awareness for the Canadian Coast Guard, Fisheries and Oceans Canada, Transport Canada and port authorities, and allied defence-and-security customers for multi-domain ISR. Zax Analytics will mature the 1a and 1b deliverables into a deployable product, with a data flywheel in which each confirmed re-identification becomes a labelled example that improves the model — a compounding advantage that incumbents tied to batch, rule-based fusion struggle to replicate. The progression path is Component 1a (Conceive, TRL 3), to Component 1b (Develop, TRL 4-5), to operational deployment and procurement (TRL 6+), positioning Canada with a sovereign, exportable multi-domain fusion capability.
