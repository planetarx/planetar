Glossary terms used in the proposal. Add each as a term and its definition.

AIS: Automatic Identification System, a transponder system that broadcasts a vessel's identity, position, course and speed.

MMSI: 
Maritime Mobile Service Identity, the unique numeric identifier transmitted by a vessel's AIS transponder.

IMO number: International Maritime Organization number, a permanent unique identifier assigned to a vessel's hull.

Dark vessel (AIS-off): a vessel that has switched off its AIS transponder to avoid being tracked.

SAR: Synthetic-Aperture Radar, satellite or airborne radar imaging that operates day or night and through cloud.

Sentinel-1: the European Space Agency's free, public C-band SAR satellite mission.

EO: Electro-Optical, visible-spectrum (camera) imagery.

RF: Radio Frequency, radio-frequency emissions used for detection and signals intelligence.

Hydrophone: an underwater microphone, used here for passive acoustic detection of vessels.

ONC: Ocean Networks Canada, the University of Victoria-operated cabled ocean observatory, including hydrophones.

Salish Sea: the coastal waters surrounding southern Vancouver Island, British Columbia.

ISR: Intelligence, Surveillance and Reconnaissance.

C2: Command and Control.

DND/CAF: Department of National Defence / Canadian Armed Forces.

TRL: Technology Readiness Level, a 1 to 9 scale measuring technology maturity.

SWaP: Size, Weight and Power, the resource constraints of edge or deployed hardware.

GBA Plus: Gender-Based Analysis Plus, the Government of Canada framework for assessing diverse population impacts.

OSINT: Open-Source Intelligence, information drawn from publicly available sources.

SOW: Statement of Work.

TA: Technical Authority.

Self-supervised learning: training a model from structure in unlabelled data; here, AIS-on co-occurrence supplies free labels.

Cross-modal fusion: combining data from different sensor types into a single model or representation.

Re-identification (re-ID): recognizing the same vessel across different observations and sensors.

Embedding: a learned numeric vector representation in which related observations lie close together.

Transformer: a neural-network architecture that uses attention over a set of inputs.

CLIP: Contrastive Language-Image Pre-training, a public method for learning a shared cross-modal embedding.

I-JEPA: Image-based Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture, a public self-supervised learning method.

Conformal prediction: a statistical method giving distribution-free confidence bounds on a model's outputs.

Provenance (lineage): the recorded chain of which inputs produced a given output, enabling audit and explanation.

Entity resolution: linking observations to a single canonical entity (a vessel) and tracking it over time.

Write-ahead log (WAL): an append-only log recording every event so the system's state can be replayed exactly.

xView3: a public dark-vessel-detection dataset and competition (Sentinel-1 SAR co-located with AIS).
